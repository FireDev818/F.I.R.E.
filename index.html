Privacy Policy

Overview

F.I.R.E. is committed to protecting your privacy. This app does not collect personal information and all user interactions are anonymous.

Camera Access

The camera permission is used only when the user chooses to attach a photo to a report. Images are stored in base64 format and submitted anonymously to Firestore.
No access occurs without the user's direct action.

Anonymous Reports

Reports are submitted without user names, emails, or device IDs. We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication only to validate usage and prevent abuse.

Firebase Usage

We use Firebase services (Firestore, Authentication, App Check) to protect the app and ensure valid usage. Your data is not shared with third parties.

Your Rights

You may remove the app at any time. All reports older than 24 hours are automatically deleted from the database.

Policy Updates

This policy may change as new features are added. Updates will be reflected on this page immediately.
